**NHL27Kit 1.0.0: a modding kit for NHL 27 on PS5 (KytyPS5)**

NHL27Kit is a Windows modding tool for NHL 27 (PPSA34063) running on KytyPS5. It edits your game dump in place, and every change is listed on a Mods page where you can Restore it.

**What it does**
🏒 **Roster:** edit players, bios and ratings, and see their in-game portraits. **NHL sync** pulls current rosters from the NHL API (trades, signings, call-ups), and you can set lines.
👕 **Uniforms in 3D:** replace any kit texture or logo, recolour helmets and gloves, and add, move or rename jersey slots. There's also a legacy-jersey converter.
🎨 **Jersey Studio:** design your own jersey from the game's create-a-team templates. Pick a pattern, colour every zone, and drag the crests, numbers and names around.
🏟️ **Teams:** names, abbreviations, menu, broadcast and rink colours, home arenas and every logo style.
🧊 **Centre ice from TheFaceoff.net:** pick any rink from their archive (NHL, AHL, ECHL, NCAA…) and the kit turns it into the game's centre-ice logo and red line, with a live preview and a saturation slider.
🖼️ **Textures & 🔊 Sounds:** browse, export and replace any texture, music track, crowd sound or commentary line.
📦 **Mod packs:** export your mods as a `.nhl27mod` file and share them. Others load it with Import pack.
🎮 **Play:** launch the game on KytyPS5, tweak the emulator settings, and get new NHL-fork builds.

**Setup**
1. Unzip anywhere and run `NHL27Kit.exe`. You don't need to install .NET.
2. Copy `oo2core_9_win64.dll` from your **FMT** folder next to the exe. It's required and can't be shipped with the kit. Copy `opus.dll` too if you want commentary audio.
3. In Settings, pick your NHL 27 dump folder (the one with `eboot.bin`, `Data`, `Patch`).
4. **Back up your game folder first**, and close the emulator before applying mods.
